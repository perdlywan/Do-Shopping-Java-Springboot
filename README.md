# 🛒 Do Shopping

Application for managing product inventory, orders, and sales reports.

### Why Is This System Needed?
This ensures accurate, effective, and controlled management of stock and transaction data, from checking stock when a customer places an order, stock reduction, payment, to managing the shipping process. Reporting features can also be used to analyze business performance.

### What are the benefits of the system?
1. Helps manage stock and transactions accurately, quickly, and in a controlled manner, thereby minimizing recording errors and the risk of running out or stockpiling goods.

2. Improves operational efficiency because ordering, payment, and stock reduction processes are automated and integrated.

3. Stored sales data can be used to generate reports and analyze product performance, allowing management to make more informed business decisions based on data.

## ⚙️ Roles & Authorization
1. Admin
  - Manage product data
  - Manage product category data
  - Manage orders data
  - Dashboard(Low Stock Alerts, Slow Moving Products)
  - Report(Sales, most-sold product, customers with the most purchases)
    
2. Customer
  - Get all product categories
  - Get all products
  - Manage shipping addresses
  - Get my orders
  - Cancel the order
  - Do payment
